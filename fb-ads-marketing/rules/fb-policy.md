# Facebook Advertising Policies

Core policies for Facebook ad compliance.

## 1. Personal Attributes

**Policy**: Ads must not contain content that asserts or implies personal attributes.

**Prohibited**:
- "Are you struggling with debt?"
- "You deserve better insurance"
- "People with diabetes hate this"

**Allowed**:
- "For people struggling with debt"
- "Better insurance options available"
- "Diabetes management solutions"

## 2. Discriminatory Practices

**Policy**: Ads must not discriminate against people.

**Prohibited**:
- Excluding audiences based on race, ethnicity, religion
- Housing ads that exclude protected classes
- Employment ads with age/gender restrictions
- Credit ads targeting specific demographics

**Allowed**:
- Location-based targeting
- Interest-based targeting
- Behavioral targeting (with limitations)

## 3. Misleading Content

**Policy**: Ads must not contain deceptive, false, or misleading content.

**Prohibited**:
- Exaggerated claims ("Cure all diseases")
- False urgency ("Last chance" when it's not)
- Misleading imagery (stock photos as testimonials)
- Deceptive landing pages

**Allowed**:
- Substantiated claims with evidence
- Real testimonials with proper disclaimers
- Accurate product representation

## 4. Restricted Content

**Categories requiring special permissions**:

| Category | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Alcohol | Age-targeted, country-specific |
| Dating | Pre-approval required |
| Gambling | Licensed operators only |
| Healthcare | Restricted targeting |
| Financial services | Pre-approval required |
| Political ads | Authorization required |

## 5. Prohibited Content

**Never allowed**:
- Tobacco products
- Recreational drugs
- Weapons/explosives
- Adult content
- Illegal products/services
- Surveillance equipment
- Body shaming content

## 6. Landing Page Requirements

**Must have**:
- Clear connection to ad content
- Functional website
- Privacy policy
- No excessive pop-ups
- No malware or deceptive content

## 7. Intellectual Property

**Policy**: Ads must not violate third-party rights.

**Prohibited**:
- Using brand names/logos without permission
- Copyrighted music without license
- Trademarked slogans in ad copy

## 8. Text in Images

**Recommendation**: Keep text minimal (<20% of image)

**Best practices**:
- Use text overlays sparingly
- Primary text in ad copy, not image
- Logo placement is acceptable
- Call-to-action buttons preferred

## 9. Political and Social Ads

**Requirements**:
- Authorization required for political ads
- "Paid for by" disclaimers mandatory
- Targeting restrictions apply
- Ad Library transparency

## 10. Data Usage

**Policy**: Ads must comply with data usage policies.

**Requirements**:
- Proper consent for data collection
- Clear privacy policy
- No unauthorized data sharing
- GDPR/CCPA compliance

## Enforcement

| Violation Level | Consequence |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Minor | Ad rejection |
| Moderate | Account warning |
| Severe | Account suspension |
| Repeated | Permanent ban |

## Appeal Process

1. Review rejection reason
2. Fix policy violation
3. Resubmit ad
4. If denied, submit appeal through Business Manager
5. Allow 48-72 hours for review
